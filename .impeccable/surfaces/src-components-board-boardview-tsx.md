# Surface: Planning boards (the shortlist wall)

Mode: Operate. Mainly the partner who plans visually (simple layout, phone, sofa or browsing shops); the other partner uses the same boards from the full layout. Job: collect ideas for a room or project, narrow them down, and turn decided ones into real list items without retyping.

User answers (2026-10-17): both inspiration and real items on one board; a free canvas (Miro-like), not a tidy grid; boards she names herself, optionally tied to a room; contents: photos, shop links (picture, name, price), live list items, sticky notes, colour swatches. Prices show on cards in both layouts (price-tag facts, never plan/affordability). Boards are found from a Home row, room pages, and a link in the simple layout, not a fifth tab.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The board is a wall you sort left to right: three wide bands, Ideas, Maybe, Decided, on a free canvas, so moving a piece rightwards is deciding. It refuses both the endless whiteboard where nothing is ever decided and the tidy Pinterest grid where nothing can be placed.
OWN-WORLD: The Paint Chart unchanged. Bands are washes of the board's room paint, faint in Ideas, stronger through Maybe to Decided; a board with no room uses sunken limestone tones. Pieces are surface cards on hairlines (photos as prints with a surface edge, notes as warm surface squares, swatches as square paint chips with name and number, shop and item cards in row typography with tabular prices). Green only for actions. No pens, arrows or shapes.
STORY: She opens "Kitchen ideas", sees her pictures loose on the Ideas band, drags the sofa she loves into Decided, the band pulses its paint, one tap adds it to the list with its picture, price and link, and the plan prices it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone. Top bar: back, board name with room chip, Board/List switch, See all. Under it the band names, pinned while panning. The canvas fills the rest, opening on Ideas with Maybe's paint showing at the right edge. Bottom at the thumb: the add bar, Photo · Link · Item · Note · Colour, replaced by the selected piece's actions when one is selected.
FORM: The shortlist wall, rank 6 of my 7 grounded structures (dealt lead). Seed key 58e3f0dc. Raised from the declined Crouwel grid specimen: pieces snap to a faint cell grid shown only while dragging.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
